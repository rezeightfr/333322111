FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does the 4ms optimization actually work?
Our engine hooks into Windows' interrupt handling and re-prioritizes input events at the kernel level. Combined with timer resolution adjustments and process scheduling tweaks, we cut the round-trip delay from hardware event to rendered frame down to 4ms.
Is it safe for my PC?
Completely. Every change is logged, reversible, and isolated from your core Windows installation. One-click uninstall restores your system to its original state.
Which games are supported?
All DirectX and OpenGL titles benefit automatically. We ship specific tuning presets for Valorant, CS2, Fortnite, Apex Legends, Minecraft, Overwatch 2, and Rainbow Six Siege.
Will this work on my hardware?
Aether supports Windows 10 (build 1909+) and Windows 11, on Intel, AMD, and hybrid architectures. Minimum 8GB RAM recommended for optimal results.
